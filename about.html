About T-Force Contracting

Dependable residential construction, remodeling, and home improvement services for Long Island homeowners.

Our Story

Built on Hard Work and Honest Service

T-Force Contracting was founded with a straightforward goal: give Long Island homeowners a single, reliable contractor they can trust for any residential project. From small repairs to complete remodels, the T-Force team brings the same level of care and professionalism to every job.

We believe every homeowner deserves clear communication, detailed estimates, and quality workmanship. That's not a marketing promise — it's how we run every project. You'll know what's happening, when it's happening, and exactly what it costs before we start.

How We Work

Our Commitment to Homeowners

Every project is guided by the same standards — no matter the size.

Quality Workmanship

We use proven construction methods and quality materials on every project. Our work is built to last, and we stand behind it. If something isn't right, we fix it.

Honest Communication

You'll always know the status of your project. We return calls, provide regular updates, and don't disappear in the middle of a job. Questions get answered — not ignored.

Detailed Estimates

Before any work begins, you'll receive a thorough estimate covering scope, materials, timeline, and pricing. No surprise costs. No hidden fees. You approve the plan before we start.

Clean Jobsite Practices

We protect your home during construction. Drop cloths, dust barriers, and daily cleanup are standard. When we leave, the only thing different should be the finished project.

Residential Focus

We specialize in residential work — not commercial. That means we understand homeowners' concerns, timelines, budgets, and the level of care your home deserves.

Licensed & Insured

T-Force Contracting is fully licensed and insured for residential contracting work in New York. Your home and your investment are protected.
