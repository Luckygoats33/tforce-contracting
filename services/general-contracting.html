General Residential Contracting

Home repairs, renovations, and multi-trade residential improvement projects.

What's Included

One Contractor. Every Trade.

Not every project fits neatly into one category. Sometimes you need a bathroom remodeled, a closet built, and a few rooms painted — all as part of the same project. T-Force is a general residential contractor, which means we coordinate multiple trades under one contract, one timeline, and one point of contact.

  • ✅ Home repairs (drywall, plumbing, electrical, etc.)
  • ✅ Multi-room renovations
  • ✅ Basement finishing
  • ✅ Attic conversions
  • ✅ Aging-in-place modifications
  • ✅ Storm and water damage restoration
  • ✅ Code compliance updates
  • ✅ Pre-sale home improvements
  • ✅ Custom residential projects

Common Problems We Solve

Why Homeowners Hire a General Contractor

Multiple Trades Needed

Your project involves framing, plumbing, electrical, tile, painting, and flooring. Instead of hiring five different companies, one general contractor manages all of them.

Don't Know Where to Start

You know something needs to change but you're not sure what's possible or what it costs. We assess your home, discuss options, and build a plan.

Previous Contractor Left the Job

Half-finished projects, unlicensed work, and contractors who stopped returning calls. We evaluate what was done, fix what needs fixing, and finish the job.

Preparing to Sell

Real estate agents recommend specific updates to maximize sale price. We handle the most impactful upgrades quickly and cost-effectively.

Why Choose T-Force

Why Choose T-Force as Your General Contractor

Single Point of Contact

One phone number, one person managing the project. You don't coordinate between trades — we do.

Proper Sequencing

Demo before framing, framing before plumbing, plumbing before drywall. We schedule trades in the right order so nothing gets done twice.

Scope Control

We define the scope clearly before starting and communicate any changes before they happen. No surprise additions to your bill.
