Common Problems We Solve

Why Homeowners Replace Their Flooring

Worn or Damaged Floors

Scratched hardwood, cracked tile, peeling vinyl, and stained carpet. We remove the old and install new flooring that's built to handle daily life.

Water Damage

Warped boards, bubbling laminate, and soft spots near kitchens and bathrooms. We repair the subfloor and install moisture-appropriate flooring.

Outdated Materials

Old linoleum, orange oak, or carpet over hardwood. Modern flooring options offer better looks, durability, and easier maintenance.

Uneven Surfaces

Floors that slope, bounce, or have visible height changes at transitions. We level subfloors and create smooth transitions between rooms.

Why Choose T-Force

Flooring Installation Done Right

Subfloor Prep

Good flooring starts underneath. We level, repair, and prepare the subfloor so your new floors lay flat and don't develop problems later.

Clean Transitions

Where tile meets hardwood, where vinyl meets carpet — transitions make or break a floor job. We get them right.

Dust Control

Floor removal creates dust. We use containment barriers and clean as we go so the rest of your home stays livable.
