Interior Painting

Professional interior and exterior painting with thorough prep and lasting finishes.

What's Included

More Than Just a Coat of Paint

A quality paint job starts with preparation. T-Force takes the time to patch, sand, prime, and protect before any paint goes on. The result is clean lines, even coverage, and a finish that holds up for years — not months.

  • ✅ Interior wall and ceiling painting
  • ✅ Exterior house painting
  • ✅ Surface preparation (patching, sanding, priming)
  • ✅ Trim, molding, and baseboard painting
  • ✅ Cabinet painting and refinishing
  • ✅ Drywall repair before painting
  • ✅ Color consultation assistance
  • ✅ Drop cloth and masking protection
  • ✅ Final touch-up and cleanup

Common Problems We Solve

Why Homeowners Repaint

Peeling and Flaking

Moisture, age, and poor prep cause paint to peel. We strip the failing paint, fix the underlying issue, and apply coatings that stick.

Dated Colors

Builder beige, dark wood trim, and accent walls that don't work anymore. Fresh paint is the fastest way to modernize a space.

Drywall Damage

Nail holes, water stains, cracks, and dings. We repair drywall and patch surfaces smooth before painting so the finish looks flawless.

Faded Exterior

Sun, rain, and Long Island winters fade and chalk exterior paint. A fresh coat protects the siding and improves curb appeal.

Why Choose T-Force

Painting Done Right

Thorough Prep

80% of a good paint job is preparation. We fill holes, sand rough spots, caulk gaps, and prime properly before any finish coat goes on.

Clean Lines

Tight masking, steady hands, and attention to where colors meet trim, ceiling, and adjacent walls. Details matter.

Quality Materials

We use premium paints that cover in fewer coats, resist scuffing, and maintain color. Better materials save you money long-term.
