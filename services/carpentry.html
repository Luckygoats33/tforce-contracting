Carpentry

Custom trim, molding, built-ins, shelving, door installation, and structural carpentry.

The Details That Make a Home

Carpentry is what separates a finished project from a polished one. T-Force provides finish carpentry — trim, molding, built-ins, and custom woodwork — as well as structural carpentry for framing, repairs, and modifications.

  • ✅ Crown molding and base trim
  • ✅ Wainscoting and chair rail
  • ✅ Built-in shelving and bookcases
  • ✅ Custom closet systems
  • ✅ Interior door installation
  • ✅ Structural framing and repairs
  • ✅ Deck and porch carpentry
  • ✅ Window and door casing
  • ✅ Stair railings and balusters

Common Problems We Solve

Why Homeowners Need Carpentry Work

Missing or Damaged Trim

Gaps between walls and floors, unfinished doorways, and chipped molding. We install, repair, and replace trim to give rooms a finished look.

No Storage Solutions

Closets that waste space, no built-ins, and cluttered rooms. We design and build custom storage that fits your home.

Rotted Wood

Window frames, door jambs, fascia boards, and deck boards that have rotted from moisture. We cut out the rot and splice in new wood.

Structural Issues

Sagging floors, bouncy joists, cracked headers, and modifications needed for remodeling. We reinforce and repair framing to code.

Why Choose T-Force

Carpentry Done Right

Tight Joints

Miter cuts that close cleanly, scarf joints that disappear, and coped inside corners that fit. Good carpentry is measured in sixteenths.

Problem Solving

Old houses aren't square, level, or plumb. We know how to make trim look right even when the walls aren't perfect.

Finish Quality

Filled nail holes, sanded edges, caulked gaps, and paint-ready surfaces. We leave the wood ready for the final finish.
